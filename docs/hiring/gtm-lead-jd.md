## About Sophia AI Factory

Sophia AI Factory is a no-code, no-tech Revenue-as-a-Service (RaaS) platform that enables non-technical CEOs to build and scale automated affiliate marketing empires using AI-generated video content. We serve SMBs and creators across Southeast Asia.

We are a post-revenue, bootstrapped-to-seed startup with $32K+ MRR, 140+ paying customers, and a clear path to $85K MRR by November 2026. We are raising a $1.5M Series A to scale.

---

## Role Overview

As our first GTM hire, you will own all go-to-market functions: creator recruitment, agency partnerships, marketing, sales, and the referral program. You will work directly with the founder to define and execute the growth strategy from product launch to Series A scale.

This is a high-autonomy, high-impact role. You will build the GTM function from scratch.

---

## Responsibilities

- **Creator Recruitment:** Identify, recruit, and onboard video creators to the Creator Marketplace. Manage creator relationships and quality assurance.
- **Agency Partnerships:** Build and manage partnerships with digital agencies for white-label agency bundles. Develop co-selling and co-marketing arrangements.
- **Marketing:** Own content marketing (blog, SEO, social), paid acquisition strategy, and email campaigns. Produce bilingual content (Vietnamese + English).
- **Sales:** Manage the sales pipeline from inbound/outbound lead to close. Handle pricing discussions, demos, and onboarding for ENTERPRISE and MASTER tier prospects.
- **Referral Program:** Design, launch, and optimize the customer referral program. Track referral metrics and iterate based on data.
- **Analytics:** Track and report on key GTM metrics: CAC, LTV, churn, pipeline velocity, channel attribution, MRR growth.
- **Community:** Build and nurture the Sophia community on Telegram, Facebook groups, and other SEA-relevant channels.
