## 1. Tong Quan / General

### Q1: Sophia AI Video Factory la gi? / What is Sophia AI Video Factory?

**Tieng Viet:** Sophia la nen tang giup ban tao video tu dong bang tri tue nhan tao (AI). Ban chi can nhap noi dung, Sophia se tu viet kich ban, tao giong noi, va tao video hoan chinh — ban khong can biet lam video.

**English:** Sophia is a platform that helps you create videos automatically using artificial intelligence (AI). You just enter your content, and Sophia writes the script, creates the voiceover, and produces the complete video — no video editing skills needed.

---

### Q2: Toi can biet lap trinh de su dung Sophia khong? / Do I need to know coding to use Sophia?

**Tieng Viet:** Khong. Sophia duoc thiet ke cho nguoi khong biet ky thuat. Moi thu deu duoc huong dan tung buoc tren Dashboard va Telegram Bot.

**English:** No. Sophia is designed for non-technical users. Everything is guided step-by-step on the Dashboard and Telegram Bot.

---

### Q3: Sophia tao video nhu the nao? / How does Sophia create videos?

**Tieng Viet:**
1. Ban nhap noi dung (chu de video)
2. AI viet kich ban tu dong (qua OpenRouter)
3. AI tao giong noi tu nhien (qua ElevenLabs)
4. AI tao video voi nguoi trinh bay ao (qua HeyGen)
5. Video hoan chinh duoc gui cho ban

**English:**
1. You enter the content (video topic)
2. AI writes the script automatically (via OpenRouter)
3. AI creates a natural voiceover (via ElevenLabs)
4. AI creates the video with a virtual presenter (via HeyGen)
5. The complete video is delivered to you

---

### Q4: Mat bao lau de tao 1 video? / How long does it take to create a video?

**Tieng Viet:** Thuong mat 2-5 phut cho video ngan (duoi 3 phut). Video dai hon co the mat 10-15 phut.

**English:** Usually 2-5 minutes for short videos (under 3 minutes). Longer videos may take 10-15 minutes.

---

### Q5: Video co chat luong nhu the nao? / What is the video quality?

**Tieng Viet:** Video duoc tao o do phan giai Full HD (1080p), phu hop de dang len YouTube, Facebook, TikTok, va cac nen tang khac.

**English:** Videos are created in Full HD resolution (1080p), suitable for uploading to YouTube, Facebook, TikTok, and other platforms.

## 2. API Keys

### Q6: OpenRouter la gi? / What is OpenRouter?

**Tieng Viet:** OpenRouter la dich vu trung gian giup Sophia truy cap vao cac mo hinh AI thong minh (nhu GPT, Claude) de viet kich ban video cho ban. No giong nhu "bo nao" cua Sophia.

**English:** OpenRouter is a service that helps Sophia access smart AI models (like GPT, Claude) to write video scripts for you. Think of it as Sophia's "brain."

---

### Q7: Lam sao de lay API Key cua OpenRouter? / How to get an OpenRouter API Key?

**Tieng Viet:**
1. Vao `https://openrouter.ai`
2. Tao tai khoan (nhan "Sign Up")
3. Dang nhap, nhan vao ten ban goc phai tren
4. Chon "Keys"
5. Nhan "Create Key" va dat ten
6. Copy key va dan vao Dashboard Sophia

**English:**
1. Go to `https://openrouter.ai`
2. Create an account (click "Sign Up")
3. Log in, click your name at top right
4. Select "Keys"
5. Click "Create Key" and name it
6. Copy the key and paste it into Sophia Dashboard

> Xem huong dan chi tiet: [Getting Started](./getting-started.md#buoc-2a-lay-openrouter-api-key)

---

### Q8: ElevenLabs la gi? / What is ElevenLabs?

**Tieng Viet:** ElevenLabs la dich vu tao giong noi AI nghe rat tu nhien. Sophia su dung ElevenLabs de doc kich ban video bang giong nguoi that.

**English:** ElevenLabs is a service that creates AI voices that sound very natural. Sophia uses ElevenLabs to narrate video scripts with realistic human voices.

---

### Q9: Lam sao de lay API Key cua ElevenLabs? / How to get an ElevenLabs API Key?

**Tieng Viet:**
1. Vao `https://elevenlabs.io`
2. Tao tai khoan
3. Dang nhap, nhan anh dai dien goc phai tren
4. Chon "Profile + API Key"
5. Nhan bieu tuong mat de hien key
6. Copy key va dan vao Dashboard Sophia

**English:**
1. Go to `https://elevenlabs.io`
2. Create an account
3. Log in, click your profile picture at top right
4. Select "Profile + API Key"
5. Click the eye icon to reveal the key
6. Copy the key and paste it into Sophia Dashboard

> Xem huong dan chi tiet: [Getting Started](./getting-started.md#buoc-2b-lay-elevenlabs-api-key)

---

### Q10: HeyGen la gi? / What is HeyGen?

**Tieng Viet:** HeyGen la dich vu tao video voi nguoi trinh bay ao (avatar AI). Sophia su dung HeyGen de tao video co nguoi noi chuyen giong nhu nguoi that.

**English:** HeyGen is a service that creates videos with virtual presenters (AI avatars). Sophia uses HeyGen to create videos with a talking person that looks like a real human.

---

### Q11: API Key co mat phi khong? / Do API Keys cost money?

**Tieng Viet:** Cac dich vu nay deu co goi mien phi de bat dau. Khi su dung nhieu, ban can nang cap goi cua tung dich vu (OpenRouter, ElevenLabs, HeyGen) rieng.

**English:** These services all have free tiers to get started. When you use more, you'll need to upgrade each service's plan (OpenRouter, ElevenLabs, HeyGen) separately.

---

### Q12: API Key cua toi co an toan khong? / Are my API Keys secure?

**Tieng Viet:** Co. API Keys duoc ma hoa va luu tru an toan tren he thong cua chung toi. Chung toi khong chia se key cua ban voi bat ky ai.

**English:** Yes. API Keys are encrypted and stored securely on our system. We never share your keys with anyone.

## 3. Chien Dich / Campaigns

### Q13: Chien dich la gi? / What is a Campaign?

**Tieng Viet:** Chien dich (Campaign) la 1 du an tao video. Moi chien dich tao ra 1 video hoan chinh voi kich ban, giong noi, va hinh anh.

**English:** A Campaign is a video project. Each campaign produces 1 complete video with script, voiceover, and visuals.

---

### Q14: Toi co the tao bao nhieu chien dich? / How many campaigns can I create?

**Tieng Viet:** Tuy thuoc vao goi dich vu cua ban:
- BASIC: 20 video/thang
- PREMIUM: 100 video/thang
- ENTERPRISE: Khong gioi han

**English:** Depends on your plan:
- BASIC: 20 videos/month
- PREMIUM: 100 videos/month
- ENTERPRISE: Unlimited

> Xem chi tiet: [Bang Gia & Goi Dich Vu](./pricing-and-tiers.md)

---

### Q15: Chien dich bi ket / mac o trang thai "Dang xu ly" / Campaign stuck at "Processing" status

**Tieng Viet:**
1. Doi them 10 phut — doi khi he thong can nhieu thoi gian hon
2. Nhan **"Lam Moi"** (Refresh) trang Dashboard
3. Neu van con ket sau 15 phut, nhan nut **"Chay Lai"** (Retry) ben canh chien dich
4. Neu van khong duoc, lien he ho tro qua Telegram @Sophia_Bbot hoac email

**English:**
1. Wait 10 more minutes — sometimes the system needs more time
2. Click **"Refresh"** on the Dashboard page
3. If still stuck after 15 minutes, click the **"Retry"** button next to the campaign
4. If it still doesn't work, contact support via Telegram @Sophia_Bbot or email

---

### Q16: Toi co the chinh sua video sau khi tao? / Can I edit a video after creation?

**Tieng Viet:** Hien tai, ban chua the chinh sua truc tiep. Ban co the tao chien dich moi voi noi dung da sua doi. Tinh nang chinh sua video se co trong tuong lai.

**English:** Currently, you cannot edit directly. You can create a new campaign with modified content. Video editing features are coming in the future.

---

### Q17: Video duoc luu o dau? / Where are videos stored?

**Tieng Viet:** Video duoc luu tren he thong dam may (cloud) cua chung toi. Ban co the tai ve bat ky luc nao tu Dashboard hoac qua Telegram Bot (lenh `/results`).

**English:** Videos are stored on our cloud system. You can download them anytime from the Dashboard or via Telegram Bot (`/results` command).

## 4. Telegram Bot

### Q18: Bot khong tra loi khi toi gui lenh / Bot not responding to my commands

**Tieng Viet:**
1. Kiem tra ban da nhap dung ten bot: `@Sophia_Bbot` (chu B viet hoa)
2. Dam bao ban da nhan **"START"** khi bat dau
3. Thu gui lenh `/help` truoc
4. Neu van khong tra loi, doi 30 giay roi thu lai
5. Neu van khong duoc, thoat va mo lai cuoc tro chuyen voi bot

**English:**
1. Check you typed the correct bot name: `@Sophia_Bbot` (capital B)
2. Make sure you tapped **"START"** when you first opened the bot
3. Try sending the `/help` command first
4. If still no response, wait 30 seconds and try again
5. If it still doesn't work, close and reopen the chat with the bot

---

### Q19: Toi co the su dung bot tren may tinh khong? / Can I use the bot on a computer?

**Tieng Viet:** Co. Ban co the su dung Telegram tren may tinh bang cach:
- Tai ung dung Telegram Desktop tai `https://desktop.telegram.org`
- Hoac su dung Telegram Web tai `https://web.telegram.org`

**English:** Yes. You can use Telegram on a computer by:
- Downloading Telegram Desktop at `https://desktop.telegram.org`
- Or using Telegram Web at `https://web.telegram.org`

---

### Q20: Toi muon doi tai khoan ket noi voi bot / I want to change the account linked to the bot

**Tieng Viet:**
1. Gui lenh `/unlink` trong Telegram
2. Bot xac nhan da huy ket noi
3. Gui lenh `/link` de ket noi tai khoan moi
4. Lam theo huong dan nhap email va ma xac nhan

**English:**
1. Send the `/unlink` command in Telegram
2. Bot confirms the account has been disconnected
3. Send the `/link` command to connect a new account
4. Follow the instructions to enter email and verification code

## 6. Du Lieu / Data

### Q24: Lam sao de xuat du lieu? / How to export data?

**Tieng Viet:**
1. Dang nhap Dashboard
2. Vao muc **"Chien Dich Cua Toi"**
3. Nhan nut **"Xuat Du Lieu"** (Export) o goc phai tren
4. Chon dinh dang: **CSV** (bang tinh) hoac **PDF** (tai lieu)
5. File se tu dong tai ve may tinh

> Tinh nang xuat du lieu chi co o goi PREMIUM va ENTERPRISE.

**English:**
1. Log in to Dashboard
2. Go to **"My Campaigns"**
3. Click the **"Export"** button at the top right
4. Choose format: **CSV** (spreadsheet) or **PDF** (document)
5. The file will automatically download to your computer

> Data export is only available on PREMIUM and ENTERPRISE plans.

---

### Q25: Du lieu cua toi co duoc bao mat khong? / Is my data secure?

**Tieng Viet:** Co. Chung toi su dung ma hoa tieu chuan nganh (SSL/TLS) va luu tru du lieu tren he thong dam may bao mat. Du lieu cua ban khong duoc chia se voi bat ky ben thu ba nao.

**English:** Yes. We use industry-standard encryption (SSL/TLS) and store data on secure cloud infrastructure. Your data is never shared with any third parties.
