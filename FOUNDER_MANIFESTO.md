# FOUNDER_MANIFESTO.md

## Sophia Exists to Turn Non-Technical CEOs Into AI Operators

We do not sell complexity. We sell leverage.

## Principles

1. **Customer self-service beats operator heroics.** If a feature requires the founder to manually manage third-party infrastructure, it is not productized.
2. **Cash proof beats roadmap poetry.** One paying customer matters more than ten unvalidated features.
3. **BYOK protects margin.** Customers bring keys; Sophia coordinates outcomes.
4. **Protected flows are sacred.** Setup Wizard, Telegram Bot, and payment activation must never be broken.
5. **Deploy truth is SHA truth.** HTTP 200 is not enough.
6. **Docs are contracts.** If docs conflict, the Constitution decides.
7. **No fake maturity.** Do not claim SOC 2, 99.9% SLA, named testimonials, or enterprise guarantees without evidence.
8. **Vietnamese and English first.** Our customers should not need developer translation.
9. **Clean enough to move fast.** Refactor for risk and revenue, not aesthetics.
10. **Code serves the Constitution.** The Constitution is more important than the code.

## Founder Operating Cadence

- Weekly: review first-customer pipeline, support friction, money graph.
- Before deploy: verify build, tests, deploy, SHA.
- Before deleting: approve business impact, technical impact, migration path, risk.
- Before promising: check payment, support, compliance, and operational truth.

## What We Refuse

- Reintroducing Polar/PayPal for Sophia billing.
- Re-enabling GitHub Actions as production deploy without explicit decision.
- Operator-managed third-party credentials as required customer onboarding.
- Rewriting architecture before Constitution acceptance.
- Treating generated artifacts as source of truth.

## What We Build Toward

A lean AI factory where customers pay, self-configure, generate value, and grow without asking the founder to become their DevOps team.
