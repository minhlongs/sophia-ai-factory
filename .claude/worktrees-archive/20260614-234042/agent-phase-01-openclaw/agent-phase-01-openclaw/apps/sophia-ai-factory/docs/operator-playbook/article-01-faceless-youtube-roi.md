### Section 3: Quality Benchmarks — Where AI Still Lags + Where It Wins

**H2:** Quality: Where AI Holds Up, Where Humans Still Win

**AI wins:**
- Consistency (same voice/style across 100 videos)
- Multilingual (one source, 10 languages out)
- A/B test velocity (10 variants in 1 hour)

**Humans still win:**
- Cultural nuance (jokes, sarcasm, regional refs)
- Storytelling arc with emotional pacing
- Brand-defining hero pieces (manifesto videos, founder messages)

**Voice notes:**
- Honest about limits. Anti-hype rule applies — don't claim AI matches humans on storytelling.
- Frame as "use AI for volume + scaling; use humans for hero pieces"
