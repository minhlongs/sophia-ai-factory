# Kickoff Call Agenda Template

**Duration**: 60 minutes
**Attendees**: CEO + key stakeholders (customer side) + AM + SME (our side)
**Goal**: Establish relationship, align on goals, set expectations

---

## Pre-Call Checklist (AM)

- [ ] Calendar invite sent with Zoom link + dial-in
- [ ] Pre-call form sent (asks for goals, constraints, attendees)
- [ ] Internal prep doc reviewed (customer's industry, recent news)
- [ ] Demo environment loaded with placeholder data
- [ ] Recording enabled (with consent) for our records

---

## Agenda

### 0:00 - 0:10 | Intros + Agenda (10 min)

**Lead**: AM

- Round-robin introductions (name, role, one fun fact)
- Walk through today's agenda
- Confirm recording consent (if recording)
- Set ground rules: cameras optional, questions anytime

**Script**:
> "Thanks for joining! I'm {{AM_NAME}}, your Account Manager. Today we have 60 minutes to get to know each other, understand your goals, and show you around the platform. We'll record this for our internal use — is that okay?"

---

### 0:10 - 0:25 | Customer's Business + Goals (15 min)

**Lead**: Customer (CEO or designated stakeholder)

**Questions to ask**:

1. Tell us about {{COMPANY_NAME}} — what do you do, who do you serve?
2. What's the biggest marketing challenge you're facing right now?
3. What does success look like 12 weeks from now?
4. Have you tried anything like this before? What worked/didn't?
5. Who's involved in approving marketing decisions?

**Listen for**: Goals, pain points, decision-making process, past experiences

**AM notes**: Capture verbatim quotes for later reference in discovery doc

---

### 0:25 - 0:40 | Platform Walkthrough (15 min)

**Lead**: AM

**What to show**:

1. Dashboard overview (live demo, placeholder data)
2. The 5 KPIs (revenue impact, active users, uptime, support, retention)
3. Content approval workflow
4. Slack channel for communication
5. Where to find help (docs, support, escalation)

**Show, don't tell**:
- Don't lecture — let them ask questions
- Use real button clicks, not slides
- Pause after each section for reactions

**Script**:
> "Let me show you what you'll see every day. This is the dashboard..." [walk through each section]

---

### 0:40 - 0:55 | Q&A + Success Metrics (15 min)

**Lead**: Joint

**Topics to cover**:

1. Any questions about what you saw?
2. Concerns or hesitations?
3. Confirm 3 KPIs for measuring Value Realization
4. Agree on pilot structure (weekly cadence, check-in schedule)
5. Discuss timeline constraints (upcoming events, launches)

**Confirm in writing**:
- 3 agreed KPIs (write in chat)
- Next milestone dates
- Who to contact for what

---

### 0:55 - 1:00 | Next Steps + Homework (5 min)

**Lead**: AM

**Recap**:
- Discovery doc will be sent within 24 hours
- Setup begins Day 4 (we'll need X, Y, Z from you)
- First post goes live Day 8
- First weekly report Day 10

**Homework for customer**:
1. Fill out brand asset intake form (link)
2. Approve discovery doc
3. Watch Loom videos 1-2 (15 min)
4. Invite team members to Slack channel

**Script**:
> "We're going to send you a recap and homework within 24 hours. The most important thing: get us your brand assets by Day 3 so we can hit our Day 8 post launch. Any questions before we wrap?"

---

## Post-Call (AM, within 24 hours)

- [ ] Send thank-you email with recap
- [ ] Complete discovery doc with notes from call
- [ ] Schedule next milestone (Day 4 setup kickoff)
- [ ] Share recording internally (if recorded)
- [ ] Add action items to project tracker
- [ ] Update `tenants/{tenantId}/audit-log`

---

## Common Pitfalls

| Pitfall | Mitigation |
|---------|------------|
| Customer talks too much, doesn't see demo | Time-box their section, transition politely |
| Demo too long, loses attention | Keep to 15 min, focus on what matters to them |
| No clear next steps | Always end with homework + dates |
| Missing decision maker | Confirm who approves what before Day 7 |
| Customer expects instant results | Set realistic timeline expectations upfront |
