## 3. Primary Repeatable Channel: Telegram Creator Outreach

### Why This Channel?

1. **Zero cost** — no ad spend, no platform fees
2. **High intent** — reaching marketing agencies/content creators who already need video tools
3. **Scalable** — 3 DMs/day × 5 days = 15 leads/week, easily automated
4. **Trackable** — unique referral codes per creator (`?ref=outreach-tg-<slug>`)
5. **Compounding** — affiliates who convert become promoters

### Execution Formula

```
Volume × Conversion × Revenue = MRR

Daily:  3 DMs × 20% reply rate × 15% close rate = 0.09 customers/day
Weekly: 0.09 × 5 = 0.45 customers/week
Monthly: 0.45 × 4 = ~2 customers/month
MRR:    2 × $199 = ~$400/mo (BASIC tier mix)
```

### DM Template (Vietnamese)

```

tạo video AI tự động trong 5 phút thay vì 3-5 ngày thủ công.

Khách hàng hiện tại tiết kiệm 90% chi phí video ($199/tháng vs $300-1000/video).

Bạn có 10 phút demo miễn phí tuần này không? Tôi show E2E pipeline.

Link: sophia.agencyos.network
```

### Creator List Criteria

- Telegram handle with 1K–50K followers
- Niche: marketing agency, content creator, e-commerce
- Location: Vietnam or SEA
- Active in last 30 days

### Repeatability Test

To prove a channel is repeatable, we need **3 consecutive weeks** with consistent:
1. Same input volume (DMs/week)
2. Same response rate (±10%)
3. Same close rate (±10%)
4. Predictable revenue output

**Target**: Any channel hitting ≥0.5 customers/week for 3 weeks = proven repeatable.
