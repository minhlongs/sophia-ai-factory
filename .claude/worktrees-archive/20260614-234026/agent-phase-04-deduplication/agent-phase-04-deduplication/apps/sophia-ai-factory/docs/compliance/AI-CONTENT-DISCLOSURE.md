## 1. Legal Background

### US Copyright Office Position (Jan 2025)

Per the US Copyright Office Report on Copyright and Artificial Intelligence (Part 3: Generative AI Training):

> "Works that are **entirely** generated by AI without meaningful human authorship are **not copyrightable**."

The Office clarified:

- Mere prompting of an AI system is **insufficient** to establish human authorship
- Selection, arrangement, or substantial modification **with creative choice** can establish copyright
- The output's copyrightability depends on **degree of human creative input**

### Implications for SaaS Terms

If our customers expect exclusive ownership of purely AI-generated outputs, that expectation cannot be satisfied. We must:

1. Clearly communicate license scope (not ownership) for purely AI-generated outputs
2. Enable and encourage meaningful human modification for copyrightable outputs
3. Document the human creative input in our generation flow

## 3. EU AI Act Article 50 Disclosure

### 50(2) — Synthetic Content Marking

All AI-generated outputs must be machine-readable marked as artificially generated.

**Implementation**:
- C2PA manifest embedded in image/video/audio files
- Invisible watermark in text output (zero-width characters + metadata)
- Manifest URL preserved for verification

**Customer responsibility**: Cannot strip C2PA metadata from generated content (technical + contractual).

### 50(3) — Deepfake Disclosure

For AI-generated content depicting real or realistic synthetic persons:

- Pre-generation disclosure prompt (user must confirm intent)
- Default disclosure on published output: "AI-generated likeness"
- Customer must comply with deepfake disclosure laws in their jurisdiction (e.g., California, Texas, EU)

### 50(4) — Public Interest Text

For AI-generated text published to inform public on matters of public interest:

- Workflow tag required: "public-interest-content"
- Disclosure added to published content: "Generated with AI assistance, edited by [named responsible person]"
- Customer responsible for accuracy + named responsible person/entity

## 5. Customer Obligations

By using AI generation features, customers agree to:

1. **Not strip C2PA metadata** from generated content (technical + contract)
2. **Comply with applicable disclosure laws** in their jurisdiction
3. **Not represent AI output as human-authored** in contexts where this is unlawful or misleading (FTC enforcement)
4. **Indemnify** Sophia AI Factory for disclosure failures if customer disabled default disclosure
5. **Comply with deepfake laws** if generating synthetic likeness of real persons
