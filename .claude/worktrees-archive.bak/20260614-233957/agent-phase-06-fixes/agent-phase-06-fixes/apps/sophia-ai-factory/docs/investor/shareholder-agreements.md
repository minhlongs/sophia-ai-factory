## 3. Voting Rights

| Share Class | Voting Rights |
|---|---|
| Common | 1 vote per share |
| Preferred | 1 vote per share (on an as-converted basis) |
| SAFE | No voting rights until converted |

**Founder control:** Founder retains majority Common voting control through Series A, ensuring continued strategic alignment.

---

## 4. Transfer Restrictions

- **Right of First Refusal (ROFR):** Company and existing shareholders have ROFR on any proposed transfer of Common shares.
- **Co-sale (Tag-along):** If Founder sells >5% of stake, Common shareholders may participate pro-rata at the same price and terms.
- **Lock-up:** 180-day lock-up on Founder shares post-Series A (standard VC term).
- **Board approval required for:** Any transfer of >10% of outstanding shares to a single buyer.

---

## 5. Protective Provisions (Preferred)

Series A Preferred holders (and later Preferred) have protective veto rights on:
- Amendment to Articles of Incorporation that adversely affects Preferred
- Creation of senior or pari passu security classes
- Repurchase or redemption of Common shares (other than standard ESOP repurchases)
- Change of control below agreed valuation floor
- New equity issuance >10% of fully diluted shares without pre-emptive rights

**Threshold:** Majority of Preferred shares (by vote) required to exercise protective provisions.

## 7. Information Rights

- **Quarterly:** Board pack (P&L, MRR waterfall, key metrics) within 15 days of quarter end.
- **Annual:** audited financials within 90 days of fiscal year end.
- **Monthly (Series A+):** Operational dashboard (uptime, cost per run, NPS, churn) via shared dashboard.

---

## 8. Founder Vesting & Acceleration

- **Founder vesting:** 4-year schedule, 1-year cliff (standard to protect investor alignment).
- **Double-trigger acceleration:** 2x acceleration on change of control + termination without cause within 12 months.
- **Single-trigger (Board discretion):** May approve single-trigger for key Founder departures in best interest of company.

---

## 9. ESOP & Incentive Pool

- **Pool size:** 8.5% fully diluted (pre-Series A) — refreshes at each major funding round to maintain ~10-12%.
- **Annual refresh:** Board approves ESOP top-up at each funding event.
- **Exercise window:** 90 days post-employment for departing employees (standard).
- **Vesting:** 4-year schedule with 1-year cliff for all new grants.

---

## 10. Liquidation Preferences

| Event | Preferred Treatment |
|---|---|
| Sale / M&A | 1x non-participating (Series A chooses: take 1x or convert to Common) |
| IPO | Automatic conversion to Common at IPO price |
| Dissolution | 1x liquidation preference before any Common distribution |

**No participating preferred** — keeps cap table clean and founder-friendly for Series A.

---

## 11. Drag-Along / Tag-Along

- **Drag-along:** If >75% of Preferred + majority of Common vote to sell, all shareholders must sell on same terms.
- **Tag-along:** Any Common shareholder may participate in Founder or Board-approved sale at same price and terms.
- **Exemptions:** Transfers to family trusts or estate planning vehicles excluded.
