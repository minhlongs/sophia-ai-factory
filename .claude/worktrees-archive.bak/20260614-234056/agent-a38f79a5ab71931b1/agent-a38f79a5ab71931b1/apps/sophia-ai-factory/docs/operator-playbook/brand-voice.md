# Sophia Brand Voice — Locked 2026-05-17

## Identity in 1 sentence

**EN:** Sophia is the calm, expert AI partner that handles revenue-generating busywork so solo CEOs can think strategy.

**VN:** Sophia là người cộng sự AI điềm tĩnh, có chuyên môn — xử lý phần việc tạo doanh thu để CEO solo tập trung vào chiến lược.

## 4 Voice Pillars (locked per operator interview)

| Pillar | Setting | Why |
|--------|---------|-----|
| **Tone** | Expert mentor — calm, knowledge-heavy | Big4-consultant feel. Authority without pressure. |
| **Persona target** | Solo founder/CEO — time-starved, money-OK | Time > money. ROI proof > novelty. Anti-jargon. |
| **Anti-pattern** | Hype overclaim | NO "10x", NO ALL-CAPS, NO exclamation spam. Numbers must be real. |
| **Pronouns** | EN: `we` + `you` / VN: `Sophia` + `bạn` | Team feel, friendly-professional. Not "I" (too personal), not "Sophia" (too 3rd-person). |

## Sentence Rhythm

- **Hero headlines:** 5-10 words. Punchy but not breathless.
- **Sub-heads:** 12-20 words. State the value concretely.
- **Body:** Mix short (5-8 words) with medium (12-18). Avoid runs of 25+ word sentences.
- **CTAs:** 3-6 words. Action verb + object. No fluff modifiers.

---

## Quantification rule

**ALWAYS** prefer concrete numbers over vague claims.

| ❌ Avoid | ✅ Use |
|---------|-------|
| "Save hours" | "Save 4-6 hours per campaign" |
| "Lots of users" | "200+ creators in beta" |
| "Fast" | "First video in 5 minutes" |
| "Affordable" | "$60/mo Growth tier" |

If no real number available → omit, don't fabricate.

## Edge Cases & Localization Notes

### Vietnamese specifics

- Use "Sophia" as subject, "bạn" as 2nd person. Avoid "anh/chị" (too formal for product UX), avoid "mày/tao" (slang).
- Mix Vietnamese with selective English technical terms when no Vietnamese equivalent is natural: "API key", "webhook", "dashboard", "campaign". Don't force "khóa API" or "móc câu" — sounds awkward.
- Numbers: use Vietnamese number format `5 phút` not `5'`.
- Currency: write `$60/tháng` not `60 USD/tháng`.

### English specifics

- US English (not UK): "color" not "colour", "optimize" not "optimise".
- Title case for headers, sentence case for body.
- Oxford comma: yes (`script, voice, and rendering`).

### Cross-locale consistency

- Hero CTAs: maintain split-test parity in BOTH languages
- Pricing displayed in USD globally — VN locale keeps `$` not `₫`
- Time formats: `5 minutes` / `5 phút` (not `5m`, `5 min`)

## Examples to Calibrate Against

### Calm/expert benchmark (DO emulate)

- **Stripe:** "Payments infrastructure for the internet"
- **Linear:** "The system for modern software development"
- **Tone:** Confident statement. No exclamation. Concrete.

### Hype benchmark (DO NOT emulate)

- "🚀 SUPERCHARGE Your Marketing With AI! 10X Your Revenue!"
- "Game-changing AI that will REVOLUTIONIZE your business overnight"

If a sentence reads like ad copy from a Twitter thread guru — rewrite it.
