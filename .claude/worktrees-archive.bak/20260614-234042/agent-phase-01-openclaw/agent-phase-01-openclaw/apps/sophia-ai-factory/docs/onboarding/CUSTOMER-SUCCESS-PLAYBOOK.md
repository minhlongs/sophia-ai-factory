# Customer Success Playbook

**Version**: 1.0 | **Date**: 2026-06-12
**Owner**: Customer Success Lead
**Goal**: Maximize adoption, retention, expansion

---

## Lifecycle Stages

```
Onboarding (Day 0-14)
    ↓
Adoption (Week 3-8)
    ↓
Value Realization (Week 9-12)
    ↓
Renewal / Expansion (Week 12+)
    ↓
Advocacy (ongoing)
```

---

## Stage 1: Onboarding (Day 0-14)

**Goal**: First value delivered

**Owner**: AM leads, CS supports

**Activities**: See `CUSTOMER-ONBOARDING-SOP.md`

**Success criteria**:
- First post published by Day 8
- First report reviewed by Day 11
- Team trained by Day 12
- NPS ≥ 8 at Day 14 retro

**If behind**: Daily standup with AM + engineering until back on track.

---

## Stage 2: Adoption (Week 3-8)

**Goal**: Customer uses platform as part of weekly routine

### Weekly Cadence

| Day | Activity | Owner |
|-----|----------|-------|
| Monday 9am | Weekly report auto-generated | System |
| Monday 10am | Report review call (30 min) | AM + Customer |
| Tuesday | Action items from report | Customer |
| Wednesday | Content review (5 min) | Customer |
| Thursday | Optimize from insights | Joint |
| Friday | Slack check-in | AM |

### Adoption Signals

**Healthy**:
- Customer logs in 3+ times/week
- Approval turnaround <24 hours
- Asks strategic questions, not just tactical
- Invites team members to platform

**Warning signs**:
- Logs in <1 time/week
- Approval turnaround >48 hours
- Only tactical questions
- No team expansion

**If warning signs**: Schedule 30-min "adoption check-in" within 1 week.

### Mid-Pilot Review (Week 8)

**Duration**: 60 min
**Attendees**: Customer (CEO + key stakeholders) + AM + Customer Success Lead

**Agenda**:
1. Review 5 dashboard KPIs (5 min)
2. Review 3 pilot-specific KPIs (5 min)
3. Wins + challenges (15 min)
4. Adjust strategy for final 4 weeks (20 min)
5. Decision preview: continue/upgrade/pause (10 min)
6. Q&A (5 min)

**Output**: Updated plan for weeks 9-12

## Stage 3: Value Realization (Week 9-12)

**Goal**: Customer sees clear ROI

### Activities

| Week | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 9 | Deep-dive analysis on best-performing content |
| 10 | 90-day performance report (custom presentation) |
| 11 | Strategy session for next quarter |
| 12 | Final decision call + handoff |

### 90-Day Report Contents

1. Executive summary (1 page)
2. KPI achievement vs targets
3. Top 5 wins with data
4. Top 3 learnings
5. ROI calculation (revenue impact vs cost)
6. Recommendations for next quarter
7. Customer testimonials (if available)

### Value Realization Signal

Customer hits at least 2 of 3 pilot-specific KPIs → ready for renewal conversation.

If only 1 of 3 → offer 4-week extension at same discount to recover.

If 0 of 3 → honest conversation about fit, offer graceful exit.

---

## Stage 4: Renewal / Expansion (Week 12+)

**Goal**: Convert pilot customer to annual contract

### Renewal Process

1. **Week 10**: Internal discussion (AM + CS + CEO) on renewal likelihood
2. **Week 11**: Present 90-day report + renewal proposal to customer
3. **Week 12**: Decision call
4. **Week 13**: Contract signed, billing updated
5. **Week 14**: Transition to ongoing CS (no longer "onboarding")

### Renewal Pricing

Pilot discount (25-50%) expires at end of Week 12. Standard pricing applies.

**Retention tactics**:
- Lock in pilot pricing for 6 months if annual commitment
- Offer quarterly check-ins (vs monthly) for budget-conscious customers
- Bundle services (more channels, more content) for expansion

### Expansion Signals

Watch for:
- Asking about new channels
- Asking about higher content volume
- Team size growing
- Referring other companies
- Asking about API access

**When 2+ signals**: Schedule expansion call within 2 weeks.

## Stage 5: Advocacy (Ongoing)

**Goal**: Customer becomes reference + referral source

### Advocacy Activities

| Activity | When | Owner |
|----------|------|-------|
| Case study | After 6 months | Marketing |
| Referral ask | After renewal | AM |
| Public testimonial | After expansion | Marketing |
| Conference speaker intro | When relevant | CEO |

### Referral Program

- Refer 1 customer → 1 month free
- Refer 3 customers → 3 months free + case study co-marketing
- Refer 5+ customers → enterprise tier upgrade

## Churn Prevention

### Early Warning System

Monitor daily:
- Login frequency (target: 3+ per week)
- Approval turnaround (target: <24 hours)
- Slack response time (target: <4 hours)
- KPI trend (target: improving)
- NPS trend (target: stable or improving)

**Alert threshold**: 2+ metrics in red for 2+ consecutive weeks → escalate to CS Lead.

### Save Plays

When at-risk customer identified:

1. **Day 0**: CS Lead takes over from AM
2. **Day 1**: Schedule "listening call" (no selling, just listen)
3. **Day 3**: Present custom save plan (e.g., reduce scope, add training, adjust KPIs)
4. **Day 7**: Follow-up on save plan execution
5. **Day 14**: Decision: recovered or graceful exit

### Graceful Exit

If customer churns:
1. Exit interview (30 min, recorded)
2. Data export (full D1 backup sent within 7 days)
3. 6-month re-engagement window (offer to return at pilot pricing)
4. Internal retro on what we learned
5. Update churn tracking in D1
