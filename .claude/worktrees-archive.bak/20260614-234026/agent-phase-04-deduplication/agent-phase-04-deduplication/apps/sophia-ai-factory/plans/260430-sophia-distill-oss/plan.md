**Research completed:** 3 parallel streams (video gen OSS, SaaS monetization, ClaudeKit orchestration)

## Key Insights Distilled

1. **FaceFusion+Wav2Lip+TTS** = đối thủ HeyGen — Sophia đã có HeyGen integration, chỉ cần polish UX
2. **Polar LLMStrategy** = auto-meter tokens — Sophia dùng NOWPayments, thêm credit meter
3. **PostHog usage-pressure** = convert at 80% quota, NOT after trial expires
4. **Dub.co affiliate** = 20% recurring 12mo, double-sided reward
5. **ClaudeKit pipeline** = plan→code→review→test — Sophia đã dùng, chỉ cần enforce
