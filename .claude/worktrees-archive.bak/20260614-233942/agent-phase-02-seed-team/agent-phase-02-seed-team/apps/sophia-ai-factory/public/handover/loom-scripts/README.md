# Loom Recording Scripts — CEO Handover Package

**Version**: 1.0 | **Date**: 2026-06-12
**Format**: 4-6 minute Loom videos for CEO handover
**Total runtime**: ~30 minutes across 7 videos

---

## Recording Settings

- **Resolution**: 1080p
- **Frame rate**: 30 fps
- **Audio**: External USB mic recommended
- **Length cap**: 6 min per video (Loom free tier limit)
- **Background**: Neutral / blurred
- **Tone**: Calm, conversational, no jargon unless explained

---

## Video 2 — Daily CEO Workflow (4 min)

**Audience**: CEO in first 2 weeks
**Goal**: Teach 10-minute daily routine

### Script

```
[0:00 — Intro]

In this video, you'll learn your daily 10-minute routine. That's all it takes
to stay on top of your marketing.

[0:20 — Morning routine]

Every morning, do these 3 things:

1. Check Slack — we'll post daily highlights here at 9am
2. Open dashboard → Reports → Last 7 days
3. Skim the AI insights at the top

If everything looks green, you're done. If anything's red, click into it.

[2:00 — Weekly review]

Once a week, spend 20 minutes on the bigger picture:
- Trends: are numbers going up or down?
- Customer comments: what are people saying?
- Channels: which one is working best?

[3:00 — When to ask for help]

Reach out via Slack for:
- Quick questions (we respond in 2 hours)
- Strategy ideas
- New feature requests

Save email for billing or formal stuff.

[3:30 — End]

Next video: how to read your weekly report.
```

## Video 3 — Reading Your Weekly Report (5 min)

**Audience**: CEO in first month
**Goal**: Interpret the 5 KPIs

### Script

```
[0:00 — Intro]

Your weekly report arrives Monday at 9am. It has 5 key numbers. Let's walk
through each one.

[0:30 — KPI 1: Revenue Impact]

This is dollars directly attributed to our work. We measure:
- New leads we generated
- Conversions through our channels
- Revenue from those conversions

A good week for a media agency: $5,000-15,000 in attributed revenue.

[1:30 — KPI 2: Active Users]

How many real people are engaging with your content. Not bots, not you
checking. Real humans.

Target: 10%+ of your audience per week.

[2:30 — KPI 3: Uptime]

Is the platform working? 99.9% means less than 1 hour of downtime per month.
Anything below 99.5% is a problem.

[3:30 — KPI 4: Support Response]

How fast we respond to your questions. Target: 4 hours during business hours.

[4:00 — KPI 5: Client Retention]

Are your customers staying? This measures if your audience keeps coming back.

[4:30 — Wrap-up]

Green = good, yellow = needs attention, red = call us immediately.
```

## Video 4 — Channels & Content Strategy (6 min)

**Audience**: CEO + marketing team
**Goal**: Explain channel strategy

### Script

```
[0:00 — Intro]

Let's talk about how we pick which channels to focus on.

[0:30 — Why channels matter]

You can't be everywhere. We'll help you pick 2-3 channels where YOUR audience
actually lives, then go deep there.

[1:00 — How we pick]

We look at:
1. Where your competitors are winning
2. Where your customers spend time
3. What content you can realistically produce

[2:00 — Channel examples]

- LinkedIn: B2B, professional services, $50k+ deals
- Instagram: consumer brands, visual products
- TikTok: younger audiences, viral content
- Email: always (you own this channel)
- YouTube: education, long-form

[3:30 — Content calendar]

We post:
- 3-5x per week on primary channel
- 1-2x per week on secondary
- Daily email to your list

[4:30 — How we create content]

Two approaches:
1. AI-drafted: We draft, you approve. Fast.
2. You draft, we polish: You bring ideas, we refine.

You'll pick which works for your team.

[5:30 — End]

Next: how to give feedback on content.
```

## Video 5 — Feedback Loop & Approvals (4 min)

**Audience**: CEO + marketing team
**Goal**: Teach the approval workflow

### Script

```
[0:00 — Intro]

In this video, how to review and approve content in 5 minutes a day.

[0:30 — Where to find content to review]

Dashboard → Content → Awaiting Approval

You'll see a queue of posts, each with:
- The draft text
- The image / video
- Target channel + scheduled time
- AI confidence score

[1:30 — How to give feedback]

Three options:
1. ✅ Approve as-is (click green check)
2. ✏️ Request edits (click pencil, type what to change)
3. ❌ Reject (red X, tell us why)

Most clients approve 70-80% as-is. The rest get small tweaks.

[2:30 — Response time matters]

We need approvals within 24 hours, or we miss the posting window.

Pro tip: review content every morning at 9am with your coffee. 5 minutes.

[3:00 — End]

Next: when things go wrong (and how we handle it).
```

## Video 6 — When Things Go Wrong (5 min)

**Audience**: CEO + ops lead
**Goal**: Reduce panic, teach escalation

### Script

```
[0:00 — Intro]

Things will go wrong. That's normal. In this video, what to do when they do.

[0:30 — The 4 severities]

P0: Service down. We respond in 15 minutes. Rare.
P1: Big feature broken. 2 hour response. Occasional.
P2: Degraded. 24 hour response. Weekly.
P3: Small stuff. 48 hour response. Daily.

[1:30 — P0 response]

If the platform is fully down:
1. You'll get a Slack notification within 15 minutes
2. We'll create a private war room channel
3. Updates every 30 minutes
4. Resolution announcement when fixed
5. Post-mortem within 48 hours

You don't need to do anything except wait and stay in the loop.

[2:30 — What you CAN do during P0]

- Check status page: status.sophia.agencyos.network
- Read Slack updates
- Forward the war room link to your team
- NOTHING else. We've got it.

[3:30 — When to escalate past AM]

Only if:
- No response after 1 hour (P0)
- Same issue recurring 3+ times
- You feel ignored

Escalation: AM → Engineering Lead → CTO → CEO

[4:30 — End]

We're ready for problems. Don't stress.
```

## Video 7 — Pilot Wrap-Up & Next Steps (4 min)

**Audience**: CEO at week 10-11
**Goal**: Prepare for pilot conclusion

### Script

```
[0:00 — Intro]

We're nearing the end of your 12-week pilot. Let's talk about what happens next.

[0:30 — Week 10-12 activities]

- Week 10: We'll present 90-day performance report
- Week 11: Strategy session for next quarter
- Week 12: Decision call — continue, pause, or adjust

[1:30 — Decision options]

Option A: Continue with same plan. Standard pricing applies.
Option B: Upgrade tier (more channels, more content).
Option C: Downsize (focus on 1 channel).
Option D: Pause. Your data is saved for 6 months.

[2:30 — How to decide]

Look at your 5 KPIs. The question is simple:
"Are we getting ROI?"

If yes: continue or upgrade.
If no but promising: extend pilot at same discount.
If no and stalling: end pilot, take what you learned.

[3:30 — End]

No pressure either way. We want this to work for YOU.

Questions? Slack me anytime.
```
