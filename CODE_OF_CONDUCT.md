# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct / Quy tắc Ứng xử Cộng đồng Mã nguồn mở

## 1. Our Pledge / Cam kết của Chúng tôi

We as members, contributors, and leaders of the **Sophia AI Factory Open-Core Community** pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Chúng tôi — các thành viên, người đóng góp và đội ngũ điều hành cộng đồng mã nguồn mở **Sophia AI Factory** — cam kết xây dựng môi trường hợp tác văn minh, minh bạch, tôn trọng và không quấy rối cho tất cả mọi người, bất kể trình độ kinh nghiệm, quốc tịch, giới tính hay hoàn cảnh cá nhân.

---

## 2. Our Standards / Tiêu chuẩn Ứng xử

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other contributors and self-hosters.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, architectural viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive code-review feedback.
- Respecting the Open-Core boundary (`Apache-2.0` Community Core vs. `Sophia Commercial / FSL-1.1` Managed Cloud & Enterprise RaaS modules).
- Never committing plaintext API keys, customer BYOK credentials, or private tokens to issues, discussions, or pull requests.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information, API keys, or workspace credentials without explicit permission.
- Intentionally bypassing or stripping copyright, license headers (`LICENSE`, `LICENSE-COMMERCIAL.md`), or security disclosures.

---

## 3. Enforcement & Reporting / Báo cáo Vi phạm

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers via `SECURITY.md` or security contact channels. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
