## Step-by-Step: Create Your First Video from URL

### Step 1: Prepare Your Product URL

Any e-commerce URL works:
- Shopify stores (`yourstore.myshopify.com/products/...`)
- WooCommerce (`yourstore.com/product/...`)
- Amazon (`amazon.com/dp/...`)
- Custom stores (`yourstore.com/products/...`)

**Pro tip:** Make sure your product page has:
- ✅ High-quality images (AI will extract these)
- ✅ Clear product description (for script generation)
- ✅ Customer reviews (for social proof)
- ✅ Price visible (for CTA optimization)

### Step 2: Choose Your AI Video Platform

**Key Features to Look For:**

| Feature | Why It Matters |
|---------|----------------|
| URL parsing | Auto-extracts images, text, pricing |
| E-commerce templates | Pre-optimized for product ads |
| 90-second format | Perfect for TikTok, Reels, Shorts |
| Bulk generation | Create 20+ videos from catalog |
| Direct publishing | Post to social platforms instantly |

### Step 3: Paste URL and Select Template

Most platforms work the same way:

1. **Paste URL** into the input field
2. **AI scans** your product page (5-10 seconds)
3. **Choose template** from preview options:
   - TikTok vertical (9:16)
   - Instagram square (1:1)
   - Facebook landscape (16:9)
   - YouTube Shorts (9:16)

**Template Selection Tips:**
- Match template to your primary platform
- Consider your audience (Gen Z = TikTok style, Millennials = Instagram)
- Test multiple templates for A/B testing

### Step 4: Customize (Optional)

AI generates a complete video, but you can tweak:

**Text Overlays:**
- Hook headline (first 3 seconds)
- Key benefits (bullet points)
- Call-to-action (final frame)

**Visual Settings:**
- Color scheme (match your brand)
- Font selection (readable on mobile)
- Transition speed (fast = energetic, slow = premium)

**Music & Voice:**
- Background music genre
- AI voiceover (optional)
- Sound effects

**Pro tip:** Start with AI defaults for your first video. Iterate after seeing performance data.

### Step 5: Generate and Download

Click "Generate Video" and wait 30-90 seconds.

**What you'll get:**
- MP4 file (ready to upload)
- Multiple format options (if platform supports)
- Thumbnail image
- Shareable link (for review)

**Download options:**
- 1080p (recommended for ads)
- 720p (faster upload, smaller file)
- 4K (if available, for high-end displays)

**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 90 seconds max

- Text overlays (many watch muted)
- Trending audio (if available)

**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 90 seconds max

- Minimal text (clean look)

- Add to Reels + Stories

### Facebook Ads from URLs

**Format:** 1:1 square or 16:9 landscape
**Best practices:**
- Problem-solution structure
- Social proof prominent
- Clear value proposition
- Strong CTA with urgency

### YouTube Shorts from URLs

**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 60 seconds max
**Best practices:**
- Fast-paced editing
- Bold text (mobile viewing)

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### ❌ Mistake 1: Using Low-Quality Product Images

**Problem:** AI can only work with what it extracts
**Fix:** Update product photos before generating videos

### ❌ Mistake 2: Generic Templates

**Problem:** Your video looks like everyone else's
**Fix:** Customize colors, fonts, and brand elements

### ❌ Mistake 3: Weak Call-to-Action

**Problem:** Viewers don't know what to do next
**Fix:** Always end with clear CTA ("Shop Now", "Learn More", "Get 20% Off")

### ❌ Mistake 4: Skipping A/B Testing

**Problem:** Never know what converts best
**Fix:** Always test 2-3 variations per product

### ❌ Mistake 5: Not Tracking Performance

**Problem:** Can't optimize what you don't measure
**Fix:** Use UTM parameters, track CTR and conversions
