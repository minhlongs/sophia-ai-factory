## The 5-Act Structure of High-Converting 90s Ads

### Act 1: The Hook (0-5 seconds)

**Goal:** Stop the scroll. Make them watch.

**Hook formulas that work:**

| Formula | Example |
|---------|---------|
| **Problem Callout** | "Still struggling with [pain point]?" |
| **POV Statement** | "POV: You finally found a [solution]" |
| **Shocking Stat** | "90% of people [wrong behavior]" |
| **Before/After** | Show dramatic transformation |
| **Pattern Interrupt** | Unexpected visual or statement |

**Pro tips:**
- First frame MUST have text overlay (40% watch muted)
- Use bold colors, faces, or movement
- Ask a question they can't scroll past

### Act 2: The Problem (5-20 seconds)

**Goal:** Agitate the pain. Make it personal.

**Structure:**
1. Name the problem specifically
2. Show who it affects (relatable person)
3. Highlight consequences of inaction

**Example script:**
> "You've tried everything. Serums, creams, expensive treatments. But your skin still looks tired. You avoid mirrors. You cancel plans. You're losing confidence."

**Visual approach:**
- Show frustration (person looking in mirror)
- Use before photos
- Add text overlays with pain points

### Act 3: The Solution (20-50 seconds)

**Goal:** Introduce your product as THE answer.

**Structure:**
1. Reveal product dramatically
2. Explain how it works (simple terms)
3. Highlight 2-3 key benefits (not features)

**Example script:**
> "Introducing GlowC Vitamin C Serum. One drop transforms dull skin in 7 days. Clinically proven. Dermatologist tested. Visible results guaranteed."

**What to show:**
- Product beauty shots (rotating, close-up)
- Application demo (if relevant)
- Before/after comparison
- Benefit icons with text

### Act 4: Social Proof (50-70 seconds)

**Goal:** Remove doubt with external validation.

**Social proof types (ranked by impact):**

| Type | Impact | Example |
|------|--------|---------|
| **Customer results** | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | "I saw results in 3 days!" + photo |
| **Expert endorsement** | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Dermatologist recommendation |
| **Review highlights** | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | "4.9 stars from 2,000+ reviews" |
| **Media mentions** | ⭐⭐⭐ | "Featured in Vogue, Elle, Allure" |
| **User count** | ⭐⭐⭐ | "Join 50,000+ happy customers" |

**Pro tip:** Use specific numbers, not vague claims.
- ✅ "4,847 five-star reviews"
- ❌ "Thousands of happy customers"

### Act 5: Call-to-Action (70-90 seconds)

**Goal:** Tell them exactly what to do next.

**CTA components:**
1. Clear action verb
2. Incentive/urgency
3. Risk reversal

**Example CTAs:**

| CTA Type | Script |
|----------|--------|
| **Discount** | "Shop now - 20% off first order. Ends tonight." |
| **Free Trial** | "Try free for 30 days. No credit card needed." |
| **Guarantee** | "Buy today. Love it in 30 days or full refund." |
| **Limited Stock** | "Only 47 left. Order before midnight." |

**Visual requirements:**
- CTA button or text on screen
- Website URL visible
- QR code (optional, for mobile)

## Templates: Plug-and-Play 90s Formulas

### Template 1: Problem-Solution (Best for New Products)

```
0-5s:  [HOOK] Call out the problem
5-20s: [PROBLEM] Agitate the pain
20-50s: [SOLUTION] Introduce product
50-70s: [PROOF] Customer results
70-90s: [CTA] Clear next step
```

**Use when:** Your audience knows they have a problem

### Template 2: Before-After (Best for Visible Results)

```
0-5s:  [HOOK] Show dramatic after result
5-15s: [BEFORE] Show the struggle
15-45s: [TRANSFORMATION] How it happened
45-70s: [PROOF] More customer results
70-90s: [CTA] "Get your results"
```

**Use when:** Results are visual and verifiable

### Template 3: Story-Driven (Best for Brand Building)

```
0-5s:  [HOOK] "This founder discovered..."
5-30s: [STORY] Origin/mission
30-60s: [PRODUCT] What they created
60-80s: [IMPACT] Customer transformations
80-90s: [CTA] "Join the movement"
```

**Use when:** Your brand story is differentiated

### Template 4: Demo-First (Best for Innovative Products)

```
0-5s:  [HOOK] Product in action (wow moment)
5-25s: [DEMO] How it works step-by-step
25-50s: [BENEFITS] Why this matters
50-70s: [PROOF] Reviews/expert endorsements
70-90s: [CTA] "See it yourself"
```

**Use when:** Your product needs explanation

## Music & Pacing: The Hidden Conversion Levers

### Music Selection by Platform

| Platform | Best Tempo | Energy Level |
|----------|------------|--------------|
| TikTok | 120-140 BPM | High energy |
| Instagram Reels | 100-120 BPM | Upbeat |
| Facebook | 80-100 BPM | Moderate |
| YouTube | 90-110 BPM | Balanced |

**Pro tips:**
- Match music to your audience demographic
- Avoid copyrighted music for paid ads
- Use platform audio libraries (free + licensed)

### Pacing Guidelines

| Section | Shot Duration | Energy |
|---------|---------------|--------|
| Hook | 1-2 seconds per shot | Maximum |
| Problem | 3-5 seconds per shot | Building |
| Solution | 5-8 seconds per shot | Peak |
| Social Proof | 4-6 seconds per shot | Sustained |
| CTA | 8-10 seconds (single shot) | Clear |

**Rule of thumb:** Faster pacing = younger audience, slower = premium feel

## A/B Testing Your 90s Ads

### What to Test (in order of impact)

**Test 1: Hook Variations** (Highest impact)
- Version A: Problem-focused hook
- Version B: Benefit-focused hook
- Version C: Curiosity hook

**Test 2: CTA Variations**
- Version A: "Shop Now"
- Version B: "Get 20% Off"
- Version C: "Try Free"

**Test 3: Social Proof Type**
- Version A: Customer reviews
- Version B: Expert endorsement
- Version C: User count

**Test 4: Music/Tone**
- Version A: Upbeat/energetic
- Version B: Calm/premium
- Version C: No music (voiceover only)

### Testing Framework

```
Week 1: Launch 3 hook variations ($20/day each)
Week 2: Kill loser, test 2 CTAs with winner ($30/day each)
Week 3: Scale winner to full budget
Week 4: Start new test cycle
```

**Success metrics:**
- CTR > 1.5% (Facebook/Instagram)
- CTR > 2.0% (TikTok)
- CPA < product price / 3
- ROAS > 3.0

## Common 90s Ad Mistakes

### ❌ Mistake 1: Weak Hook

**Symptom:** <20% view-through at 10 seconds
**Fix:** Front-load the most interesting visual/statement

### ❌ Mistake 2: Feature Dumping

**Symptom:** Drop-off at 30-50 second mark
**Fix:** Focus on benefits, not features

### ❌ Mistake 3: Vague CTA

**Symptom:** High views, low clicks
**Fix:** Add specific, urgent CTA with incentive

### ❌ Mistake 4: No Text Overlays

**Symptom:** Low engagement from muted viewers
**Fix:** Add text to every key moment

### ❌ Mistake 5: Skipping Social Proof

**Symptom:** High interest, low trust
**Fix:** Add customer results, reviews, or endorsements
