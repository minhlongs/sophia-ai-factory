## 1. ICP PERSONAS

### Persona A — Agency Owner (Primary Decision Maker)
**Demographics**
- Title: Founder/CEO/Managing Director
- Company: SEA digital agency, 5-50 headcount, $500K–$5M revenue
- Location: Vietnam, Thailand, Philippines, Indonesia, Singapore
- Age: 30–45, tech-comfortable but time-starved

**Pain Points**
- Spends 4–8h/proposal → kills billable hours
- Loses deals to faster competitors
- Junior staff writes weak proposals → low close rates
- No consistent proposal quality across team

**Jobs-to-be-Done**
- Close more deals without hiring more sales staff
- Impress clients on first contact
- Free up senior time from admin to strategy

**Decision Triggers**
- Just lost a deal because competitor submitted faster
- Q1/Q2 revenue target pressure
- Hiring freeze → need leverage not headcount

**Best Channels:** LinkedIn direct, founder Slack communities, word-of-mouth from peers

---

### Persona B — Sales Director (Champion)
**Demographics**
- Title: Head of Sales / Business Development Manager
- Reports to: Agency Owner
- KPI: Monthly closed revenue, proposal-to-close ratio

**Pain Points**
- Proposal pipeline bottleneck → can't scale outreach
- Inconsistent messaging across proposals
- Relies on designer/copywriter → 2–3 day turnaround
- Can't personalize proposals at scale

**Jobs-to-be-Done**
- Hit monthly quota without increasing team size
- Submit proposals same day as client inquiry
- Track which proposals convert and why

**Decision Triggers**
- Monthly quota review approaching
- Boss asks why close rate is below 30%
- Just discovered competitor using AI proposals

**Best Channels:** LinkedIn, sales-focused newsletters (Sales Hacker SEA), agency conferences

---

### Persona C — Marketing Manager (Influencer/User)
**Demographics**
- Title: Marketing Manager / Content Lead
- Involved in: Pitch decks, case studies, brand voice in proposals
- Concern: Does AI maintain brand consistency?

**Pain Points**
- Manually reformatting proposals for each client
- Brand guidelines ignored by sales team
- No visibility into what content wins deals

**Jobs-to-be-Done**
- Ensure proposals reflect brand voice
- Repurpose winning proposal content for marketing
- Reduce back-and-forth with sales on copy edits

**Decision Triggers**
- Sales submits off-brand proposal to major client
- Asked to create proposal templates but no time
- Sees competitor case study about AI-powered pitches

**Best Channels:** Marketing communities, Product Hunt, Twitter/X, AI tool newsletters

## 4. REFERRAL PROGRAM SPEC

### Mechanics
**Program Name:** Sophia Partner Circle
**Model:** Double-sided reward (referrer + referee)

| Action | Referrer Gets | Referee Gets |
|--------|--------------|--------------|
| Referee signs free trial | — | 14-day trial (extended from 7) |
| Referee converts to paid | 1 month free ($99–$999 value) | 20% off first 3 months |
| Referee stays 3 months | $100 cash via bank transfer | — |

**Tier upgrade:** Refer 3 paying customers → permanent 10% lifetime discount

### Tracking
- Unique referral link per user (generated in dashboard)
- UTM parameter: `?ref={user_id}`
- Attribution window: 30 days from first click
- Dashboard shows: clicks, trials, conversions, rewards earned
- Tech: Polar.sh webhooks → custom referral table in Supabase

### Rewards Delivery
- Account credits: auto-applied at next billing cycle
- Cash rewards ($100): manual bank transfer within 7 business days
- Email notification on each milestone

### Launch Plan
**Pre-launch (D1–D7):**
- Build referral tracking in dashboard
- Write referral program landing page
- Create email template for referral invite

**Launch (D8):**
- Email blast to all trial users
- LinkedIn post announcing partner program
- In-app banner: "Earn free months. Refer a friend."

**Activation (D9–D30):**
- Personal outreach to top 10 most-engaged trial users → ask them to refer
- Leaderboard email (week 2): show top referrers
- Referrer spotlight: feature top referrer on LinkedIn (social proof + motivation)

**KPIs:** 20% of trial users share link | 5% conversion from referred trials | target 5 paid referrals in 30 days
