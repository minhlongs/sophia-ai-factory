```

- Source: Content, ads, referrals, events

- Action: Lead scoring, nurture sequence

Stage 2: Qualification (SQL)
- Source: Demo request, trial signup
- Criteria: Budget, authority, need, timeline
- Action: Discovery call, needs assessment

Stage 3: Demo/Trial
- Source: Qualified leads
- Criteria: Active trial usage
- Action: Product demo, use case alignment

Stage 4: Proposal
- Source: Demo-completed trials
- Criteria: Expressed interest, budget confirmed
- Action: Custom proposal, pricing discussion

Stage 5: Negotiation
- Source: Proposal sent
- Criteria: Contract review, legal/security questions
- Action: Address objections, finalize terms

Stage 6: Closed Won
- Source: Signed contract
- Criteria: Payment processed
- Action: Onboarding kickoff, success handoff
```
