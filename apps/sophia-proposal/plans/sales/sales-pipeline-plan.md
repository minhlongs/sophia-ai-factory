## Outreach Scripts

### LinkedIn Connection Request

```
Hi [Name], I noticed you run [Agency Name] — impressive work with [recent project]!

I'm building Sophia AI, a tool that helps agencies like yours create proposals in 30 seconds instead of 20 hours.

Would love to connect and share insights about agency growth.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### Follow-Up 1 (Value-first)

```
Hi [Name], thanks for connecting!

I noticed [Agency Name] specializes in [niche]. I just published a guide on "How to Scale Agencies from $1M to $5M" — thought you might find it useful:

[Link to blog post]

No pitch, just value. Let me know what you think!

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### Follow-Up 2 (Soft pitch)

```
Hi [Name], hope you're having a great week!

Quick question: How long does it typically take your team to create a winning proposal?

We just helped [Similar Agency] reduce their proposal time from 20 hours to 30 seconds using AI. Happy to share how if you're interested.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### Demo Request

```
Hi [Name], based on our conversation, I think Sophia AI could save your team 10+ hours/week on proposals.

Would you be open to a 15-min demo? I'll show you:
1. How AI generates proposals in 30 seconds
2. Video pitch creation (HeyGen integration)
3. ROI calculator for your specific situation

Here's my calendar: [Calendly link]

Looking forward to it!
[Your Name]
```
