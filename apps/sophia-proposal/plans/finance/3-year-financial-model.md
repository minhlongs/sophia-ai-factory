## Unit Economics

### Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)

| Tier | ARPU | Lifespan | LTV |
|------|------|----------|-----|
| Starter | $49/mo | 24 mo | $1,176 |
| Growth | $149/mo | 30 mo | $4,470 |
| Premium | $499/mo | 36 mo | $17,964 |
| Master | $999/mo | 42 mo | $41,958 |

**Blended LTV:** $18,000 (weighted average)

### Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

| Channel | Spend | Customers | CAC |
|---------|-------|-----------|-----|
| Content Marketing | $50K | 200 | $250 |
| Paid Ads | $100K | 200 | $500 |
| Referrals | $25K | 100 | $250 |
| Community | $25K | 100 | $250 |

**Blended CAC:** $350 (weighted average)

### LTV:CAC Ratio

```
LTV:CAC = $18,000 : $350 = 51:1
```

**Industry benchmark:** 3:1
**Sophia AI:** 51:1 (extremely efficient)

### Payback Period

```
Payback = CAC / (ARPU × Gross Margin)
Payback = $350 / ($500 × 0.85)
Payback = 0.8 months
```

**Target:** <12 months
**Sophia AI:** <1 month (excellent)
