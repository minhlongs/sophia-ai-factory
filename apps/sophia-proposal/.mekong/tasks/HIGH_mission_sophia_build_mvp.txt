--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**HeyGen API:**
- Base URL: https://api.heygen.com/v1
- Auth: API Key (X-Api-Key header)
- Endpoints:
  - POST /video/generate — Create video task
  - GET /video/status — Check status
  - GET /video/url — Get download URL

**HubSpot API:**
- OAuth2 authentication
- Scopes: contacts.read, contacts.write, deals.read
- Rate limit: 100 requests/second
- Sync frequency: Real-time (webhook) + hourly (batch)

**MCU Pricing:**
- Intro video (30s): 100 MCU
- Section video (60s): 250 MCU
- Full proposal (2-3min): 500 MCU
- Custom: 100 MCU/minute
- Tier discounts: Growth 10%, Premium 20%, Master 30%

RISKS + MITIGATIONS

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
