--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROLLBACK PLAN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Trigger Conditions:**
- Error rate >5% for 15+ minutes
- Payment flow broken
- Data corruption detected
- Security breach suspected

**Rollback Steps:**
1. Identify issue severity
2. Notify team (Slack #incidents)
3. Revert to last known good commit
4. Deploy rollback (git revert + push)
5. Verify services restored
6. Post-mortem scheduled
