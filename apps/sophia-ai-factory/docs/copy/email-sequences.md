### Email 2 -- First Video Generation Tips (Day 3)

**Subject:** Make your first video 3x better with these tips
**Tiêu de:** Lam video dau tien tot hon 3 lan voi nhung meo nay

---

**EN**

**Before**
Your first video generated. Maybe the voice sounds a bit robotic. Maybe the visuals do not match exactly what you imagined. That is normal -- raw AI output is rarely perfect on the first try.

**After**
With small tweaks, that same video can look polished, sound natural, and feel like your brand. The difference is not money or time. It is knowing which levers to pull.

**Bridge**
Here are three tips that make the biggest difference:

**1. Write a specific script, not a topic**
Instead of "Introduce my company," write:
> "We are [Company]. We help [Customer] achieve [Result]. Here is how it works..."

Specific scripts produce tighter, more relevant videos.

**2. Choose the right voice**
In ElevenLabs, pick a voice that matches your brand personality. Professional, friendly, energetic -- test 2-3 voices before committing. A voice that fits your brand makes your video feel 10x more polished.

**3. Use the "Tweak Prompt" button after generation**
Your first draft is a starting point. Click "Tweak Prompt," adjust the scene description or tone, and regenerate. Each iteration gets closer to what you want.
