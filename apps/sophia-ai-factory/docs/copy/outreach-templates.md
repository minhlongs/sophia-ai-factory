## Usage Notes

- **Personalize every message.** Replace [Name], [Ten], [channel topic], [client name] with actual details before sending.
- **LinkedIn DM:** Keep under 300 characters for the preview window. Lead with the first sentence as the hook.
- **Email subject lines:** Keep under 50 characters for mobile display. Use Vietnamese for VI versions, English for EN versions.
- **Telegram:** Use shorter paragraphs. Telegram readers scroll fast. Bold key numbers and CTAs.
- **Follow-up cadence:** Space messages at least 48 hours apart. Day 3, Day 7, Day 14. Do not send all three in one week.
- **Tracking:** Use UTM parameters on all links: `?utm_source=outreach&utm_medium=[linkedin|email|telegram]&utm_campaign=agency-partner-[month][year]`
- **Test first:** Send to 5-10 prospects. Track open rate and reply rate. Adjust messaging before scaling.
