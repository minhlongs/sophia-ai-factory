## Selection Rationale

### Primary Factors

1. **Cost Efficiency**
   - BARR's pricing aligns with startup budget constraints
   - Provides excellent value without compromising audit quality
   - Transparent pricing model with no hidden costs

2. **Startup-Specific Expertise**
   - BARR specializes in high-growth SaaS startups
   - Familiar with modern tech stack (Next.js, Vercel, Supabase, Cloudflare)
   - Understands startup operational realities and rapid iteration

3. **Ecosystem Reputation**
   - Strong relationships with YC, a16z, Techstars
   - Recommended by numerous startup founders and investors
   - Portfolio includes successful SaaS companies at our stage

4. **Balanced Timeline**
   - 5-7 weeks is reasonable for business needs
   - Fast enough to meet investor/customer requirements
   - Not so rushed that it compromises thoroughness

5. **Founder Experience**
   - Clear, jargon-free communication
   - Educational approach for first-time audits
   - Responsive customer service

### Secondary Considerations

- **Future scalability**: BARR can support SOC 2 Type II and other frameworks as we grow
- **Investor alignment**: Many of our investors have portfolio companies that use BARR
- **Technical fit**: Experience with cloud-native, serverless architectures (our stack)
- **Quality assurance**: While less expensive than enterprise auditors, maintains high professional standards
