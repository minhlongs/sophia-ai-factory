## 2. Three High-Conversion Script Blueprints
### Blueprint 1: "Exclusive Fee Discount & Signup Bonus" (Spot/Futures Kickback)
- Archetype: Direct Transactional Value. Format: 45-60s Short-form (9:16) & 3-5m Long-form (16:9).
- Hook (0-5s): "Stop throwing 30% of your trading profits directly into exchange fees. Watch this before opening another order."
- Problem/Proof (5-20s): Show fee tier calculator. Trader doing $500k monthly volume bleeds $300-$500 in taker fees. Contrast with 20-30% lifetime kickback code.
- Demo/KYC Walkthrough (20-35s): Screen capture: Insert partner referral code during registration -> Confirm "You receive 20% commission rebate" tag -> 60s Fast-track ID verification -> Claim $1,000-$5,000 deposit tier reward.
- Compliance In-Video (35-45s): Visual bottom-third overlay: "Past performance != future returns. Capital at risk. Paid partnership."
- CTA (45-60s): "Lock your lifetime fee rebate via link in bio/description before registration closes."

### Blueprint 2: "Trading Bot & Copy Trading Blueprint" (Automated Strategy)
- Archetype: Social Proof & Systemic Automation. Format: 60-90s Vertical & 6-8m Deep Dive.
- Hook (0-5s): "How automated spot grid bots capture sideways crypto volatility 24/7 without checking charts every 5 minutes."
- Strategy Architecture (5-30s): Explain upper/lower price grid bounds, order density, and automated arbitrage between grid lines. Show actual backtested runtime PnL & Sharpe ratio.
- Risk/Drawdown Disclosure (30-50s): Crucial trust builder. Show worst-case scenario: Max Drawdown (MDD) when price breaks below lower grid. Explain stop-loss parameters and liquidation risks in copy trading.
- Platform Execution (50-70s): Navigate exchange copy-trading/bot terminal -> Filter master traders by 90-day win-rate, MDD < 15%, and profit sharing % -> Allocate risk budget.
- CTA & Disclaimer (70-90s): "Clone this bot setup or copy top verified traders via partner portal link below. Only risk capital you can afford to lose."

### Blueprint 3: "Exchange Feature / Airdrop / Staking Tutorial" (Yield & Launchpool)
- Archetype: Low-Friction Utility & Web3 Growth. Format: 60s Fast Guide (9:16).
- Hook (0-6s): "Get allocated brand-new tokens before public trading goes live. Step-by-step guide to Exchange Launchpool."
- Mechanism & Math (6-25s): Stake BNB/USDT/FDUSD -> Hourly pool reward distribution -> Explain APY calculation vs pool dilution. Contrast with locked staking risks.
- UI Walkthrough (25-45s): Mobile app screen capture: Navigate to "Launchpool" -> Commit stablecoin -> Snapshot schedule -> Harvest tokens to Spot wallet.
- Risk Buffer (45-52s): "Rewards fluctuate based on total pooled capital. Impermanent loss & lockup terms apply."
- CTA (52-60s): "Create your KYC-verified account through the exclusive link in description to participate in the upcoming launchpool."

## 4. Video Architecture: 15s End-Card & Caption Overlay Specifications
- **Real-Time Caption Overlay (0:00 - End)**:
  - Placement: Persistent bottom-third banner (margin-bottom: 96px for 9:16 safe zone; margin-bottom: 48px for 16:9).
  - Visual: Semi-opaque dark card (`rgba(10, 11, 14, 0.85)`), border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
  - Content: `[Sponsored/Affiliate] Risk Warning: Crypto trading involves substantial loss risk. 70-80% retail lose capital. NFA.`
- **Mandatory 15s End-Card Screen (Final 15 Seconds)**:
  - Layout: Static full-frame compliance card with subtle ambient dark glow. Zero distracting fast cuts.
  - Required Text (WCAG AA Contrast ≥ 4.5:1):
    ```
    IMPORTANT FINANCIAL & LEGAL DISCLOSURE
    • Past performance is not indicative of future results. Simulated results have inherent limitations.
    • Cryptocurrency derivatives and trading involve substantial risk of loss and are not suitable for every investor.
    • This content is for educational purposes only and does not constitute financial, investment, or legal advice.
    • The creator receives compensation when you register via links provided (FTC 16 C.F.R. § 255).
    • Restricted in jurisdictions where prohibited (US derivatives restricted persons, Singapore, Vietnam residents excluded).
    ```
  - Audio: Audio bed dips to -18dB; synthetic voiceover or clean silence reciting core risk warning.
