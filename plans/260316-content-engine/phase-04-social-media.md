## Appendix: Platform Guidelines

### Twitter/X
- **Length:** 280 chars (single), 2-5 tweets (thread)
- **Tone:** Conversational, witty, timely
- **Hashtags:** 2-3 per post
- **Best times:** 9-11 AM, 2-4 PM weekdays

### LinkedIn
- **Length:** 1000-1300 chars
- **Tone:** Professional, insights, thought leadership
- **Hashtags:** 3-5 per post
- **Best times:** 8-10 AM, 5-6 PM weekdays

### Instagram
- **Length:** 2200 chars max (aim 150-300)
- **Tone:** Visual-first, inspirational, authentic
- **Hashtags:** 5-10 per post
- **Best times:** 11 AM-1 PM, 7-9 PM
