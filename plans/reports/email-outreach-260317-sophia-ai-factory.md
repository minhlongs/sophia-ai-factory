| Demo no-show | -10 lead score | Send replay + reschedule offer |
| Proposal viewed | +20 lead score | Send follow-up Email 3.3.1 |
| Proposal signed | Convert to customer | Stop all outreach, start onboarding |

### 5.2 Lead Score Thresholds

| 30-49 | 🟠 Warm | Newsletter + content drip |

### 5.3 Unsubscribe/Optimization Rules

| Event | Action |
|-------|--------|
