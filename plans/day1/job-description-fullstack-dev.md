## 🎯 About Sophia AI Video Factory

Sophia AI Video Factory is building the future of e-commerce video advertising. We turn product URLs into 90-second video ads in under 2 minutes using AI.

**Launch Target:** Day 30 Public MVP
**Current Stage:** Day 1 — Founding Team Hire
**Stack:** Next.js 14, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL (Supabase), Shotstack API, Polar.sh, Shopify API
**Deploy:** Vercel (frontend), Cloudflare Workers (API)
