# Sophia AI Factory Company Blueprint

**Prepared:** 2026-10-15

**Planning horizon:** six months from approval (15 October 2026–14 April 2027)

**Company stage:** Zero → Problem-Solution Fit

## Executive Summary

Sophia is a B2B SaaS + Revenue-as-a-Service (RaaS) platform for non-technical Vietnamese agency and SMB operators who need repeatable AI video campaigns. AI video is the entry wedge; broader revenue automation is an expansion hypothesis. Customers configure their own third-party API keys and integrations through the product. Sophia operates the platform itself.

The founder confirmed zero paying customers. The current business profile records $0 MRR as of 2026-10-15. Therefore, demand, retention, acquisition cost, gross margin, and repeatability remain unvalidated. The company’s $1M ARR path is a directional goal, not a forecast or current run rate. Unsupported July investor data-room traction claims are excluded from this blueprint because they are not supported by the canonical business records reviewed.

**Pricing and billing truth:** BASIC $199/month, PREMIUM $399/month, ENTERPRISE $799/month, MASTER $4,999 one-time; NOWPayments (USDT) and PayOS (Vietnam domestic) are the only customer billing providers. Verify any usage/MCU limits against the live canonical tier configuration before presenting them to customers.

**Evidence labels used below:** Verified means stated by a current canonical source or confirmed by the founder; Hypothesis means it needs customer or operating evidence; Target means a proposed goal, not an achieved result; Not evidenced means the reviewed source set does not prove the claim.

### Readiness and Evidence Rules

- **Stage:** Zero → Problem-Solution Fit, founder-confirmed zero paying customers; MRR $0 as recorded in the refreshed company profile. Total users are not verified.
- **IPO score:** 1/10 evidence-backed internal baseline. The score reflects a documented platform and governance controls; it does not claim legal readiness, audited financials, board oversight, or public-company controls. Re-score only against a published rubric and actual evidence.
- **Financial claims:** no current CAC, LTV, churn, payback, or gross margin is established. Track actuals before publishing these values. BYOK moves third-party AI spend to customers but does not make support, payment, and platform costs zero.
- **Product and deployment claims:** the repository documents capabilities and a live production URL; this work does not verify individual flows or live-edge SHA. Follow the production verification contract before making a current deployment claim.
- **Pricing:** the canonical payment-pricing source governs. Customer-facing copy must remain bilingual and consistent with the current tier configuration.
